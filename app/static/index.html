iCloud → Immich

Copy your iCloud photos and videos into your own Immich server and check that every file arrived intact. Then, only if you want to, free up the space in iCloud and on your iPhone.

This page needs its access key

To keep other people on your network out, this app only works when the link includes its access key: the long code after #t= at the end of the link.

This is not your Immich API key or your Apple password. You'll enter those in the next steps.

Where to find it:

  • On a server (Docker, TrueNAS, Unraid…): open the app's logs and look for the line that starts with http:// and contains #t=. Open that link, or copy the part after #t= into the box below.
  • On Windows or Mac: close this tab and open the app again. It opens the right page by itself.

This browser will remember the key, so next time the plain address is enough.

Connect your Immich server

How do I get an API key?
  1. Open Immich in your browser and sign in.
  2. Click your profile picture (top right), then Account Settings.
  3. Open API Keys and click New API Key.
  4. Type any name (for example "iCloud move"), leave all permissions switched on, and click Create.
  5. Immich shows the key only once. Copy it and paste it above.

Reading your iCloud library…

Copy to Immich

Nothing is deleted in this step. Each file is downloaded from iCloud, uploaded to Immich, and checked to be an exact copy. Live Photos keep their motion, edited photos keep the original underneath, and your albums are recreated.

· ·

Now open Immich and look through what arrived. When you're happy, you can choose more in step 3 or free up space in step 5.

    Free up space optional

    This deletes the copied photos and videos from iCloud, which also removes them from your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Only items confirmed as exact copies in Immich can be deleted, and each one is checked again right before it goes.

    Not sure yet? Skip this step. Your photos stay in iCloud and in Immich, and you can come back to it any time.

    One more step to actually get the space back. Deleted items wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days. On your iPhone, open Photos, scroll down to Recently Deleted (under Utilities), tap Select, then Delete All.
