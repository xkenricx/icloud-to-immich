iCloud → Immich

Copy your iCloud photos and videos into your own Immich server, check every file arrived intact, then (only if you want) free up the space in iCloud and on your iPhone.

Connect your Immich server

Where do I get an API key?

In Immich, click your profile picture → Account Settings → API Keys → New API Key. Give it any name, leave all permissions on, and copy the key it shows you.

Reading your iCloud library…

Copy to Immich

Nothing is deleted in this step. Each file is downloaded from iCloud, uploaded to Immich, and checked byte-for-byte. Live Photos keep their motion, edited photos keep their original underneath, and your albums are recreated. You can stop at any time and pick up later.

· ·

    Free up space optional

    This deletes the copied items from iCloud, which also removes them from your iPhone, iPad and Mac. Only items that are confirmed identical in Immich can be deleted. Each one is checked again right before it goes.

    One more step to actually get the space back. Deleted items wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days. On your iPhone open Photos, scroll to Recently Deleted (under Utilities), tap Select then Delete All.

    Finish

    Signs you out of iCloud on this computer, deletes the temporary sign-in data, and closes the app. Progress is kept, so if you run the app again with the same Apple ID and Immich server it carries on where it left off.

    All done. You can close this tab.
